# Privacy Policy **Effective Date: August 20, 2026** Tempoo ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Tempoo handles information when you use our music player application. Tempoo is designed to provide a simple and private music playback experience. We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect or store your personal information. ## 1. Information We Collect Tempoo does not collect, store, or sell personal information. The App does not require: * User registration or account creation * Name, email address, phone number, or other contact information * Passwords or login credentials * Location information * Contacts * Photos or personal documents * Personal identification information Tempoo does not maintain a user database or create user profiles. ## 2. Access to Music Files Tempoo may request permission to access audio files stored on your device. This permission is used solely to: * Find and display music files available on your device * Read audio file information, such as song title, artist, album, and duration * Play music selected by you * Provide music playback functionality Tempoo does not upload your music files to our servers. Your music files remain on your device and are not transmitted to us. ## 3. Music Playback Tempoo processes audio files locally on your device in order to provide music playback. Audio data is not sent to our servers for storage, analysis, or processing. ## 4. Data Storage Tempoo does not store your personal information on our servers. Any application settings or playback-related information that may be stored locally on your device is used only to provide or improve the functionality of the App and is not transmitted to us. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Because Tempoo does not collect or maintain personal information, there is no personal information to sell or share. ## 6. Internet Access Tempoo does not require an account or online service to play music stored on your device. If the App uses network access for future features or third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before such features are introduced. ## 7. Children's Privacy Tempoo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Since the App does not require an account and does not collect personal information, it is designed to minimize the collection of user data. ## 8. Data Security We value your privacy and take reasonable measures to protect the App and its operation. Because Tempoo does not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is no personal user database maintained by us. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our privacy practices. When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. ## 10.
