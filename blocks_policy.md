# petBlocks Privacy Policy

Release Date: October 17, 2026

"petBlocks" (the "Game") is developed and operated by Wuhan Mozhu Entertainment Network Technology Co., Ltd. ("we" or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

**Important: This Game is completely free. It contains NO in-app purchases, payments or recharge features. We never collect any payment accounts, bank cards or transaction data.**

## 1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require registration. We do NOT collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or any other directly identifying information.

1. **Locally stored game data**: Your level progress, coins, items and preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to our servers or linked to your identity. Uninstalling the Game or clearing app data deletes this information.
2. **Information collected by the advertising SDK**: The Game integrates Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect: Advertising ID (GAID), device model, OS version, app version, IP address, and approximate location (country/city level) for ad serving, frequency capping and measurement. Such data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to: (1) keep the Game running (local saves); (2) display ads (app open, interstitial, rewarded and native ads); (3) prevent ad fraud and measure ad performance. We do not build advertising profiles for marketing and we never sell your personal information.

## 3. Permissions

The Game only requests permissions necessary for operation: network access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for loading ads, and the Advertising ID permission (AD_ID) required by AdMob. The Game does NOT request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or phone-state permissions.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated is **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). You may reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's privacy settings to opt out of ad personalization.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents may use Google Play parental controls and system advertising settings to manage their children's experience.

## 6. Data Storage & Security

Game progress is stored locally in the app's sandbox. Advertising data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption.

## 7. Your Rights

You may delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data; you may reset your Advertising ID in system settings; and you may contact us for any questions or complaints. We will respond within 15 business days.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the Game or on this page.
