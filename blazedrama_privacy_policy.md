# BlazeDrama Privacy Policy

Release Date: November 1, 2026

"BlazeDrama" (the "App") is developed and operated by Wuhan Mozhu Entertainment Network Technology Co., Ltd. ("we" or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

**Important: This App is completely free. It contains NO in-app purchases, payments or recharge features. We never collect any payment accounts, bank cards or transaction data.**

## 1. Information We Collect

The App can be browsed as a guest, but an account is required to save your watch history and favorites. When you register or sign in, we collect:

1. **Account data**: email address, nickname, avatar URL, gender and birthday (optional). Passwords are encrypted with RSA before transmission and never stored in plain text.
2. **Usage data**: watch history, favorites and episode progress, stored on our servers and linked to your account.
3. **Locally stored data**: session tokens, cached images and app preferences are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data deletes this information.
4. **Information collected by the advertising SDK**: the App integrates Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect Advertising ID (GAID), device model, OS version, app version, IP address and approximate location (country/city level) for ad serving, frequency capping and measurement. Such data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
5. **Sign-in providers**: if you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, the provider shares your basic profile (account ID, email, nickname, avatar) with us through Firebase Authentication.

We do NOT collect your contacts, photos, SMS, microphone data or precise GPS location.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to: (1) create and secure your account; (2) provide drama playback, watch history, favorites and cross-device sync; (3) display ads and prevent ad fraud; (4) diagnose crashes and improve performance. We do not build advertising profiles for marketing and we never sell your personal information.

## 3. Permissions

The App only requests permissions necessary for operation: network access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for loading content and ads, and the Advertising ID permission (AD_ID) required by AdMob. The App does NOT request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or phone-state permissions.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated in the App:

- **Google AdMob** (Google LLC) — advertising. You may reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's privacy settings to opt out of ad personalization.
- **Firebase Authentication** (Google LLC) — account authentication and Google/Facebook sign-in.
- **Google Sign-In** and **Facebook Login** — optional sign-in providers.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

## 5. Data Storage & Security

Account and usage data are transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption and stored on secured servers. Passwords are RSA-encrypted in transit and hashed at rest. Session tokens are stored in the App's private sandbox. We retain account data for as long as your account remains active.

## 6. Your Rights

You may access, correct or delete your account data by contacting us at the email below. You may delete local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data, and you may reset your Advertising ID in system settings. We will respond to deletion requests within 15 business days. Deleting your account removes your profile, watch history and favorites.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page, and the Release Date above will be updated.
