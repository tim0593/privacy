Privacy Policy for TiOS

Last updated: September 20, 2026
Developer: SyncYuan
Contact: sandyqqu@gmail.com

TiOS is an Android launcher that provides home-screen, weather, calendar, wallpaper, app-discovery, and optional subscription features. This Privacy Policy explains how TiOS handles information when you use the app.

Information TiOS accesses

Location

If you allow location permission, TiOS accesses approximate or precise device location to provide local weather. Location coordinates and the resolved city name may be stored on your device for weather refreshes. TiOS sends location coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather forecasts. If you do not grant location permission, the weather feature may use a previously saved location or a default location instead.

Calendar

If you grant calendar permission, TiOS reads calendar events to show your schedule and lets you create, edit, or delete events that you choose to manage through the app. Calendar data is used on your device for these features. TiOS does not intentionally send calendar event content to TiOS-operated servers.

Notifications

If you separately enable notification access in Android Settings, TiOS may process active-notification information to provide notification dots and related launcher features. TiOS does not intentionally send notification content to TiOS-operated servers.

Installed apps and app usage

TiOS accesses the installed-app list to display and launch apps. If you enable usage access, TiOS may access app usage information, such as recently used apps and last-used times, to provide recent-app shortcuts. This information is processed on your device and is not intentionally sent to TiOS-operated servers.

Photos and media

If you choose an image for wallpaper or visual customization, TiOS accesses the image you select. TiOS does not intentionally upload selected images to TiOS-operated servers.

Purchases

TiOS may offer subscriptions or other in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. TiOS does not collect or store payment-card information.

Advertising, remote content, and third parties

TiOS may display ads and uses Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device and online identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostic information, and other information as described in Google's privacy documentation and your device or Google advertising settings.

TiOS also contacts TiOS-operated services to obtain remote configuration, recommended apps, content, and update information. These services may receive standard technical request information, such as IP address, device/browser information, and request logs. Recommended content may include links to third-party websites or app stores.

For optional video or content feeds, TiOS may request content from TiOS-operated services and, where applicable, Google/YouTube services.

Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and Ads
  • Open-Meteo Terms
  • YouTube / Google Privacy Policy

Data retention and deletion

Data stored by TiOS on your device, such as launcher settings, weather cache, and local feature preferences, remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Calendar events are managed in your device calendar and can be deleted through TiOS or your calendar app.

You can control permissions in Android Settings. You can stop location, calendar, notification, media, or usage-access processing by revoking the relevant permission or access. You may request information or deletion assistance by contacting us at sandyqqu@gmail.com.

Security

TiOS uses reasonable measures designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Children

TiOS is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the app or at this policy URL and update the “Last updated” date.
